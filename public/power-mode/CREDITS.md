# Power mode's guests

Pictures that fly out now and then while power mode is on (src/powerMode.tsx). None of them is Badger's own.

- **ferris.svg**: Ferris the crab, the unofficial mascot of Rust, by Karen Rustad Tölva.
  From https://rustacean.net/assets/rustacean-flat-happy.svg, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
- **bevy.svg**: the Bevy engine's bird icon, from https://bevy.org/assets/bevy_icon_dark.svg.
  **bevy-light.svg** is the same icon in darker greys, for light themes. The Bevy logo belongs to the
  Bevy project and is used here under its terms.
- **rick.gif**: a clip from the music video for "Never Gonna Give You Up" by Rick Astley (1987).
  The song and video are © their rights holders; used here as a joke, not as Badger's own.
